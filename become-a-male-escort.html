Become a male escort

Requirements, process, earnings and the platforms that put your profile online. The complete starting point.

Working as a male escort means accompanying women, men or couples for a fee. For many, the appeal is the mix of varied encounters, a schedule you control and a good income. This guide sets out, plainly, what the job involves and how to get started.

Requirements

There are no strict age limits. In practice, most escorts are roughly between 21 and 55. What matters more than age is how you present yourself and how you treat people.

  • A well-groomed appearance and a confident, friendly manner
  • Good manners and the ability to hold a conversation
  • Empathy and a reliable, respectful character
  • Enough life experience to be at ease with different people
  • Up-to-date health checks, typically a recent negative STD and HIV test

How getting started works

The route to your own profile usually follows a set pattern. Knowing it saves you time.

1

Prepare photos

Recent, well-lit shots of your face and body, neatly dressed and unfiltered. Your photos decide the first impression.

2

Register

Through the sign-up form of a specialist platform. An intake conversation usually follows.

3

Create your profile

Text, rate and contact methods are set. If you prefer, your photos can be made unrecognisable so you stay anonymous.

4

Receive enquiries

Once the profile is live, the first enquiries arrive. From here, you decide which bookings to accept.

What can you earn?

You set your own rate, and hourly fees commonly sit between about £75 and £150. Lower rates tend to bring more enquiries, higher ones fewer but more per booking. The fee is paid directly in cash, with no agency deductions, and travel costs are agreed separately. For a fuller picture, see How much do male escorts earn?

Discreet from the start

An alias instead of your real name, no banking trail and, if you wish, photos made unrecognisable. That keeps your privacy intact. Details are in the article Discretion as a male escort.

Where to sign up

For a professional profile, a specialist platform beats a general classifieds site. On a specialist platform there is a proper selection process, your profile is maintained and it is actively promoted. Well-established options for the UK and beyond include Gigolodirect and Male Escorts UK. Both are aimed at men who want to start seriously, with their own profile.

Common mistakes when starting out

  • Weak or heavily edited photos that give an unrealistic impression
  • A profile full of clichés instead of an honest description
  • Vague terms on rate and process that lead to misunderstandings
  • Being hard to reach, so enquiries go nowhere

Get these right and you have a solid foundation. The rest comes with experience.
