Cookie Policy

Short and honest: this site works almost entirely without cookies.

What we use

Max Male Escort does not use cookies for advertising or analytics. There is no tracking, no audience measurement and no sharing of data with third parties for marketing.

Essential browser storage

So that the cookie notice does not reappear on every visit, a single entry is placed in your browser's local storage after you dismiss it:

  • mmx_cookie_ok – records that the notice has been acknowledged. It holds no personal data and remains until you remove it.

This entry is purely technical and needed only for the display of the notice.

How to remove it

You can clear the entry at any time through your browser settings. In most browsers, the privacy or security settings include an option to clear site data and local storage. Once cleared, the notice will appear again on your next visit.

External fonts

Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts to display the site. This does not set cookies, but it does open a connection to a Google server. Details are in the Privacy Policy.
