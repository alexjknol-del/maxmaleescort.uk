How much do male escorts earn in the UK? A realistic guide

Published 24 June 2026 · About a 4 minute read

No question comes up more often than the one about money. The honest answer is that it depends. Earnings as a male escort vary widely, and anyone advertising fixed numbers is usually promising too much. Still, it is possible to sketch a realistic range.

The hourly rates

Typical rates sit somewhere between roughly £75 and £150 an hour. Some charge less, and a well-established few charge considerably more, reaching £200 or beyond. What matters is that the rate suits your profile rather than putting people off.

A lower rate brings more enquiries; a higher rate brings fewer bookings but more per date.

That simple rule of thumb explains most decisions. If you want to build up a steady flow of bookings quickly, starting lower often helps. Once you are established, you can raise the rate.

What drives the figure

  • The quality and recency of your photos, which decide the first click
  • An honest, well-written profile
  • Being reachable and reliable when enquiries come in
  • Your location and willingness to travel
  • How you carry yourself, and whether a first date turns into a regular client

What you keep

One important point: when bookings are arranged directly, without an agency, the fee goes to the escort in full. There is no commission and no banking trail. Payment is made in cash, in person, and travel costs are agreed separately. On specialist platforms such as Gigolodirect, the only charge is for the profile itself, not the individual booking.

Staying realistic

How many bookings actually come through is impossible to predict. Some weeks are quiet, others busy. Income is rarely as steady as a salary. Accept that, keep working on a strong profile, and you give yourself the best chance. The full route is set out in the Become a male escort guide.
