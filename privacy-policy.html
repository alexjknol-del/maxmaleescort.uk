Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters. This policy explains what data is processed when you visit this website.

1. Who we are

This Privacy Policy applies to the website maxmaleescort.uk (the site). The operator of the site is the data controller for any personal data processed through it. For any privacy question, contact info@maxmaleescort.uk.

2. Our approach to data

Max Male Escort is an information website. There is no contact form, no newsletter, no user account and no comment section. We deliberately process as little personal data as possible.

3. Hosting and server logs

The site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages, provided by Cloudflare, Inc. (101 Townsend Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA). When you load a page, the host automatically processes technical connection data such as your IP address, browser type and version, operating system, referring page and the time of the request. This is used to deliver the site and keep it secure. The lawful basis is our legitimate interest in providing a reliable and secure website (UK GDPR Article 6(1)(f)).

4. Cookies and local storage

The site sets no tracking or advertising cookies. To remember that you have dismissed the cookie notice, a single entry is stored in your browser's local storage. It contains no personal data and is not shared with anyone. Further detail is in the Cookie Policy.

5. Fonts (Google Fonts)

The site uses fonts from Google Fonts, provided by Google Ireland Limited (Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland). When a page loads, your browser requests the fonts from a Google server, which involves transmitting your IP address to Google. This is done to present the site consistently, on the lawful basis of legitimate interest (UK GDPR Article 6(1)(f)). More information is available in Google's privacy notice at policies.google.com/privacy.

6. Email contact

If you email us, we process the information you provide in your message in order to respond. We keep such correspondence only as long as needed to deal with your enquiry. The lawful basis is our legitimate interest in answering messages sent to us (UK GDPR Article 6(1)(f)).

7. External links

This site links to external websites operated by third parties. Once you follow such a link you leave this site, and the third party is solely responsible for how they process your data. We recommend reviewing their privacy policies.

8. Encryption

For security, the site uses TLS encryption. You can recognise an encrypted connection by the padlock icon in your browser's address bar and the https prefix.

9. Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to access the personal data we hold about you, to have it corrected or erased, to restrict or object to its processing, and to data portability, subject to the applicable legal conditions. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO), the UK supervisory authority, at ico.org.uk. To exercise any right, email info@maxmaleescort.uk.
