Answers at a glance

The questions people ask most about male escorting, answered clearly and briefly.

What is a male escort?

A male escort accompanies women, men or couples for a fee. That might be dinner, company at an event or a private date. The scope and boundaries are always agreed between both sides beforehand.

Is working as a male escort legal in the UK?

Escorting itself is legal in the UK when carried out within the law. Certain related activities, such as soliciting in a public place or running a brothel, are offences. Every escort is responsible for understanding and following the rules that apply to them.

How does a booking work?

The client chooses a profile and makes contact directly. Date, location, duration and fee are then discussed, and payment is made in person and in cash. The How it works page describes the whole process.

How much do male escorts earn?

Hourly rates commonly sit between about £75 and £150, sometimes higher. Actual earnings depend on your profile, your rate and how many bookings you take. There is a detailed breakdown in the news section.

Will my identity stay private?

Yes. It is normal to use an alias instead of your real name, to have photos made unrecognisable if you prefer, and to be paid in cash with no banking trail. That keeps privacy protected on both sides.

What are the requirements?

A well-groomed appearance, good manners, empathy and reliability. There are no strict age limits, though in practice most escorts are between 21 and 55. Up-to-date health checks are standard.

Where can I sign up as a male escort?

For a professional profile, specialist platforms such as Gigolodirect or Male Escorts UK are the usual choice. The Become a male escort guide explains the process in detail.

Does Max Male Escort arrange bookings?

No. Max Male Escort is purely an information guide, not an agency. We do not manage profiles or handle any bookings.
